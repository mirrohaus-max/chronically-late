What we do

The core services under MIRRØHAUS.

Everything we offer plugs into the same creative system, so your LLC, brand, website, and even your books all feel like they belong to the same world.

Brand Systems & Creative Direction

Naming, positioning, and visual identity that give your brand a clear lane. We design full systems — story, rules, and assets — that feel real and last across fashion, lifestyle, ecommerce, publishing, and whatever comes next.

Digital & Ecommerce

Websites, funnels, CX mapping, and launch strategy for brands that want to sell, not just look good. Clean, modern builds that match the brand world — no generic templates, no noisy clutter.

LLC & Business Formation

Simple, guided LLC setup for founders who want to start correctly. Name checks, Articles of Organization, EIN, Operating Agreement, and optional brand + website launch bundles under one roof.

Book Publishing & IP Studio

Concept to bookshelf. We help structure your book, align it with your brand, oversee design and layout, and guide you through publishing platforms like KDP and IngramSpark so your story becomes an asset, not just a project.

Retainers

Retainer systems for founders who want a creative department, not random freelancers.

These retainers are for serious builders only — founders who want an ongoing partner to help with brand, digital, LLC, and publishing moves over time.

Tier 1 — Lane Support

A light monthly partnership for founders who need ongoing eyes on the brand without a full rebuild.

  • Monthly strategy check-in (1x call)
  • Light brand & website tweaks
  • Creative direction on campaigns or launches
  • Basic LLC / compliance questions and guidance
  • Email access for quick decisions

Best for early-stage or single-brand founders.

Tier 2 — System Partner

Deeper involvement in your ecosystem: brand, site, funnels, and IP all moving together.

  • Monthly strategy + implementation calls (2x)
  • Ongoing design & copy updates for web and campaigns
  • Launch support for new products, drops, or offers
  • LLC & business formation add-ons for new ventures
  • Support on book or content projects tied to the brand

Best for growing brands with multiple moving pieces.

Tier 3 — Ecosystem Studio

A full creative system in your corner. Brand, web, launches, LLC moves, and books all run through one studio so everything stays on-brand.

  • Weekly strategy & execution calls
  • Priority design & development queue
  • Multi-brand or multi-site support under one umbrella
  • Hands-on LLC formation and new-venture rollout planning
  • End-to-end book publishing support (outline to launch)

Best for founders building an actual system of brands.
