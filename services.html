Services

MirroHaus is built for founders who want a premium, consistent presence across every brand touchpoint. You can work with us on a specific project or through an ongoing retainer.

Core Services

  • Brand strategy, naming, and positioning
  • Logo and visual identity systems
  • Website & landing page design
  • Funnel and conversion layout
  • Launch creative for new products or brands
  • Ongoing creative direction and asset design
  • Book cover design and publishing support

Retainer Tiers

Tier I – Foundation

For solo founders or lean teams who need a dedicated creative arm without hiring in-house.

  • Monthly design & copy support
  • Site tweaks and landing page updates
  • Prioritized small projects

Tier II – Growth

For brands running active campaigns, launches, or multiple product lines.

  • Everything in Tier I
  • New page and funnel builds
  • Ongoing creative strategy sessions

Tier III – System

For full ecosystems: multiple brands, complex funnels, or publishing + e-commerce under one roof.

  • Priority access and rapid turnarounds
  • Multi-brand creative direction
  • Launch planning & asset roadmaps

Book Publishing Support

MirroHaus also supports authors and brand leaders who want to publish books that match the level of their brand. That can include:

  • Cover design and interior layout
  • Brand-aligned landing pages and funnels
  • Basic launch creative for social and email

If you're planning a book as part of your brand strategy, mention it when you reach out.
